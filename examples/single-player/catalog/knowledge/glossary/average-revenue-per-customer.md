Recurring revenue divided by the number of customers behind it, over active subscriptions.

"ARPU" is the term people reach for and it is the one worth being careful with, because the U is a
USER and this catalog has two candidates for that word. `revenue_per_customer` divides by distinct
customers; `mean_subscription_mrr` averages the subscription-months themselves. A customer holding
three subscriptions is one of the first and three of the second, so the two answers differ by
however much the base fans out.

When a question says ARPU and the difference could matter, ask which one is meant rather than
picking.
