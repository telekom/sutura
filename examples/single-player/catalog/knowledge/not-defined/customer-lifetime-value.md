There is no certified definition of customer lifetime value here, and its absence is a decision
rather than a gap somebody has not got round to.

Every version of it needs three things this catalog does not hold: an expected lifetime, a discount
rate, and a decision about which costs to net off. Any of the three chosen differently gives a
different number, and all three are commercial decisions rather than modelling ones.

So a question asking for CLV gets told that plainly, together with what does exist -
`revenue_per_customer` for what a customer is worth in a month, and `churn_rate` for how quickly
subscriptions end. Composing those two into a lifetime value is exactly the step that needs the
decision, and it is not one for whoever is answering the question.
