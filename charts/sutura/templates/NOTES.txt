{{ include "sutura.fullname" . }} is deployed.

Single player only: every query on this deployment runs as the one identity `config.base`
declares for its sources. `security.inbound` (if you configured it) tells this process WHO
is asking; it does not make a source execute AS that caller.

Check readiness:

  kubectl get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}

Ask a certified question, from inside the cluster:

  kubectl run -it --rm curl --image=curlimages/curl --restart=Never -- \
    curl -sf -H "Authorization: Bearer <security.access_token>" \
    http://{{ include "sutura.fullname" . }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}/v1/query \
    -d '{"metric": "...", ...}'

`GET /health` on the same Service and port needs no token; it is what `startupProbe` and
`livenessProbe` already ask.
