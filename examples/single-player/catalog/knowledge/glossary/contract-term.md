Whether a subscription is on a rolling month-to-month arrangement or a committed one: the two values
are `monthly` and `annual`, and both may be filtered on.

Worth not confusing with the period a question asks about. "Monthly revenue for annual contracts"
is the `month` grain over a filter of `annual`, and both halves of that sentence use a word the
other one could have claimed.
