Churn in this catalog is subscription-level. Customer-level churn has no definition here.

The question it needs answered first is what it means for a customer to churn: losing every
subscription they held, losing their last one in a product family, or falling below some threshold
of spend. Those are three different numbers, and the choice belongs to whoever owns the figure
rather than to whoever is asked for it.

`churn_rate` and `subscriptions_churned` are what exist, and the caveat on both says what they
count. Reporting either of them as customer churn is the specific mistake this note is here to
prevent.
