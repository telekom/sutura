Private households, as opposed to companies. The value is `consumer`.

The third value of that dimension is `wholesale`, which is the traffic sold on to other operators
rather than to anybody's customer. Nobody has a nickname for it, so it has no entry of its own -
but a question about "everybody except business" covers two values and not one, and the honest
answer groups by `segment` rather than filtering on a guess.
