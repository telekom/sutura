Each region rolls up into one sales area, and this catalog records that with no date beside it. A
breakdown by sales area files every month of a region's history under the area it rolls up into as
recorded: a region moved to another area takes all of its earlier months with it.

So a trend by sales area shows how the regions are grouped as recorded, not how they were grouped
each month. Say so when a question asks how a sales area changed over time.
