Nothing here forecasts anything. Every metric in this catalog reports a period that has already
happened, out of rows that were written when it did.

A question about next quarter therefore has no answer here, and the useful reply says so and offers
the history instead: `recurring_revenue` by month over the periods that exist is the input somebody
would forecast FROM, and producing the forecast is separate work with its own review.

Extrapolating the trend and presenting the result beside a certified figure is the failure this note
exists to prevent. The number would carry the version and digest of definitions that never produced
it, which is worse than an uncertified number - it is an uncertified number wearing a provenance
record.
